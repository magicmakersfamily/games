For grown-ups

Tap the microphone and say one word. It shows the word, its syllables and sounds when they are in our hand-checked word bank, a picture when available, and reads everything back — to help with sounding out words while learning to read.

Magic Makers doesn't store your audio or words. Speech recognition is handled by your browser and may use its own speech service to turn your voice into text.
