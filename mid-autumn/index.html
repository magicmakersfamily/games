Mid-Autumn Mayhem

Time時間6:00 pm
Celebrating 0
Reunion points 0
Speed速度0.75×

The full moon is rising over the harbour.圓月正在海港上空升起。

It is the 15th night of the 8th lunar month: the Mid-Autumn Festival. Tonight everyone wants to be together under the round moon. Build the party from 6 pm to midnight.今天是農曆八月十五，中秋節。今晚大家都想在圓月下團聚。由晚上六點到午夜，一起佈置派對吧。

  • Use + and − to add buildings, people, lanterns, mooncakes, fruit and more.用 + 和 − 加入大樓、人、燈籠、月餅、生果等等。
  • Every happy, celebrating person earns Reunion points.每個開心慶祝的人都會帶來團圓分。
  • Watch the six trouble meters. If one fills up, trouble arrives: fire trucks, ambulances, police, monkeys…留意六個麻煩指數。任何一個滿了，麻煩就會來：消防車、救護車、警察、馬騮……
  • Tap surprise visitors before they disappear. Point at any Chinese word to hear it.趁驚喜訪客未離開前點一下。指著任何中文字就可以聽到讀音。
  • Too much food makes a stink. Tap poop to clean it up; tea helps too.食物太多會發臭。點一下便便就可以清理，喝茶也有幫助。
  • 8 secret surprises are hidden in the city. Tap signs, teapots, boats, hills… and see what happens!城市裡藏著 8 個驚喜。點點招牌、茶壺、船、山……看看會發生甚麼事！
  • Too fast? Use Speed − to slow the night down.太快？用「速度 −」令晚上慢一點。
