The full moon is rising over the harbour.

It is the 15th night of the 8th lunar month: the Mid-Autumn Festival. Tonight everyone wants to be together under the round moon. Build the party from 6 pm to midnight.

  • Use + and − to add buildings, people, lanterns, mooncakes and more.
  • Every happy, celebrating person earns Reunion points.
  • Watch the four trouble meters. If one fills up, trouble arrives: fire trucks, ambulances, police…
  • Tap surprise visitors before they disappear. Point at any Chinese word to hear it.
