        Tank journal

        What happened, and why

        About Takashi Amano (1954–2015). A Japanese photographer and aquarist who invented the “Nature Aquarium”: underwater gardens that look like real landscapes and work like tiny ecosystems. The Amano shrimp is named after him — he made it famous as an algae eater. In this game the chemistry is simplified so a week fits in a few seconds, but the cause-and-effect is real.
