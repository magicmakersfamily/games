Why these brushing steps?Based on current guidance from the American Dental Association and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.
  • Brush twice a day for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Clean the outside, inside and chewing surfaces of every tooth.
  • Use short, gentle, tooth-wide strokes, and tilt the brush up and down behind the front teeth.
  • After brushing, spit out the extra toothpaste and rinse very little or not at all, so the fluoride stays on the teeth.
  • Use the right amount of toothpaste: a smear the size of a grain of rice under age 3, and a pea-sized amount from 3 to 6. Young children need a grown-up to help or watch. If your child can’t spit yet, keep to these small amounts rather than rinsing.

Sources: ADA brushing guide, home oral care and fluoride for children; AAPD fluoride therapy. Based on guidance published by the ADA and AAPD; neither organization has reviewed or endorsed this app. Brushing the tongue is our own extra step.
